# Cerner SurgiNet — Surgical and Perioperative Workflow Management
# MODULE: Clinical
# SYNTHETIC KNOWLEDGE BASE — Cerna RAG System

## What Is SurgiNet in Cerner?

SurgiNet is Cerner Millennium's perioperative information system, designed to manage the full surgical care continuum from pre-operative scheduling through intraoperative documentation to post-anesthesia recovery and surgical follow-up. It is the primary clinical application for surgical nurses, anesthesiologists, operating room (OR) coordinators, and surgical technicians.

SurgiNet integrates directly with PowerChart, eMAR, BCMA, and the CPOE order entry system to provide a seamless perioperative clinical workflow within the Cerner Millennium platform. The OR tracking board, anesthesia documentation, nursing care plans, and case scheduling are all centralized in SurgiNet.

## SurgiNet Core Components

### 1. OR Tracking Board (Surgical Case Board)
The OR tracking board provides a real-time view of all surgical cases, room assignments, case status, and staff assignments. Similar to the FirstNet ED tracking board, it displays:
- Active cases by OR room
- Surgeon, anesthesiologist, and scrub/circulating nurse assignments
- Case status (Pre-Op, Patient In Room, Procedure Ongoing, Case Closing, PACU)
- Estimated case time vs. actual elapsed time
- Room turnover status between cases
- On-call cases and add-on case requests

### 2. Pre-Operative Module

#### Pre-Op Assessment Documentation
Nurses complete the pre-operative assessment PowerForm in SurgiNet before the patient enters the OR:
- Surgical history and previous anesthesia reactions
- Current medications (cross-referenced with medication reconciliation)
- Allergies (verified against BCMA allergy list)
- NPO (nothing by mouth) status confirmation and time
- Pre-op vital signs (baseline)
- Lab and imaging results review
- Consent verification and documentation
- Site marking confirmation (laterality, level, surgical site)

#### Pre-Op Checklist
SurgiNet presents a configurable **pre-operative safety checklist** aligned with the WHO Surgical Safety Checklist:
- Patient identity verification (two-factor: name + date of birth or MRN)
- Correct procedure, correct site, correct patient confirmation
- Consent signed and in chart
- Pre-op antibiotics administered (timing documented within 60 minutes of incision)
- Beta-blocker or aspirin management per protocol
- VTE prophylaxis ordered
- Implants/special equipment confirmed available

### 3. Intraoperative Documentation Module

#### Intraoperative Nursing Notes
The circulating nurse documents real-time intraoperative events:
- Patient in room time, anesthesia start, incision time, closure time, patient out time
- Positioning (supine, prone, lateral, lithotomy) and protective padding
- Instruments, sponge, and needle counts (opening, interim, and closing counts)
- Implants used (device lot numbers, UDI codes for regulatory compliance)
- Specimen handling and labeling for pathology
- Blood products administered (linked to blood bank and BCMA)
- Electrosurgical settings and pad site skin integrity

#### Anesthesia Documentation
Anesthesia providers document directly in SurgiNet's anesthesia record:
- Anesthesia type (general, regional, MAC, local)
- Airway management details (intubation, LMA, tube size)
- Continuous vital sign capture (many ORs have automated vital sign feeds)
- Anesthetic agents administered (gases, IV medications)
- Fluid balance (crystalloids, colloids, blood products)
- Regional block documentation with medication details
- Emergence and extubation notes

#### Instrument and Sponge Counting
SurgiNet supports automated and manual count workflows:
- **Manual count**: scrub and circulating nurse count and document at opening, intermittently for long cases, and at closing
- **Electronic counting** (with RFID-capable systems): RFID-tagged sponges and instruments scanned for automated count verification
- Discrepancy alert: if closing count does not match opening count, SurgiNet fires a mandatory alert — case cannot proceed until discrepancy is resolved or documented with imaging verification

### 4. PACU (Post-Anesthesia Care Unit) Module

After the patient leaves the OR, PACU nurses use SurgiNet for post-anesthesia recovery documentation:
- Aldrete scoring (post-anesthesia assessment scale) or other discharge scoring systems
- Vital sign monitoring (continuous or periodic)
- PACU pain assessment and medication administration (via eMAR/BCMA link)
- Nausea and vomiting management
- Emergence delirium documentation
- Phase 1 (intensive monitoring) and Phase 2 (step-down) PACU distinction
- PACU discharge criteria met: score threshold and physician order required
- Transfer documentation to inpatient unit or same-day surgery discharge

## SurgiNet Case Scheduling

### Surgical Scheduling Integration
SurgiNet's scheduling module manages the surgical slate (OR schedule):
1. Surgeon office requests case via **SurgiNet Scheduling Portal** or direct scheduling staff entry
2. Scheduling coordinator reviews:
   - Surgeon's block time or open scheduling
   - Room availability and estimated case duration
   - Required equipment, implants, special teams
3. Case is confirmed and appears on the OR tracking board
4. Patient receives pre-op instructions and appointment confirmation
5. Pre-admission testing (PAT) orders auto-generated

### Block Scheduling
- Surgeons are assigned **block time** — reserved OR time for their cases
- Block time management: release unused blocks (typically by 5 PM the day before)
- Released blocks become open for other surgeons or emergency add-ons
- Block utilization reports in **SurgiNet Analytics > OR Utilization Dashboard**

## BCMA Integration in SurgiNet

Medications administered intraoperatively are documented via the BCMA scanning workflow even in the OR setting:
1. Anesthesia provider or circulating nurse scans patient wristband
2. Scans medication barcode
3. Documents route and dose in SurgiNet medication administration record
4. eMAR/BCMA records are part of the unified medication administration history in PowerChart

For high-alert medications (neuromuscular blocking agents, concentrated electrolytes, opioids), the dual-nurse verification process may be configured — same as inpatient BCMA settings.

## SurgiNet Administration and Configuration

### Accessing SurgiNet Administration
1. Navigate to **Cerner Solutions > SurgiNet Administration**
2. Or launch SurgiNet > Administration tab (requires SurgiNet admin security role)

### Configuring OR Rooms and Resources
1. **OR Room Configuration**: define physical OR rooms, capacity, specialty designation
2. **Equipment Resource Types**: MRI-compatible equipment flags, robot-assisted surgery designation
3. **Staff Role Configuration**: define OR-specific staff roles (scrub, circulator, anesthesia tech, OR coordinator)
4. **Block Schedule Templates**: configure weekly block templates by surgeon or surgical specialty

### Configuring Intraoperative Forms
1. Navigate to **PowerForms Administration > Intraoperative Documentation**
2. Customize nursing notes sections for different surgical specialties
3. Configure sponge and instrument count workflows
4. Set implant/device documentation requirements (UDI capture fields)

### Configuring PACU Scoring
1. Navigate to **SurgiNet Administration > PACU Configuration**
2. Select scoring system (Aldrete, Modified Aldrete, MPADSS)
3. Configure minimum score thresholds for PACU discharge
4. Set vital sign monitoring frequency defaults

## Key SurgiNet Metrics and Reporting

SurgiNet feeds the perioperative analytics dashboards:
- **OR utilization**: % of scheduled block time actually used
- **First case on-time start**: % of first cases starting within 15 minutes of scheduled time
- **Turnover time**: average time between one case out and next case in
- **Surgical site infection (SSI) rate**: linked to infection prevention reporting
- **Implant tracking**: regulatory reporting for device recalls

Access OR analytics: **Discern Analytics > Perioperative Performance Dashboard** or **SurgiNet Analytics > OR Utilization**

## SurgiNet Safety and Compliance

- All documentation is time-stamped and user-attributed for joint commission compliance
- The WHO Surgical Safety Checklist workflow is configurable and enforcement can be mandatory
- SurgiNet supports surgical case documentation export for NSQIP (National Surgical Quality Improvement Program) reporting
- Implant/UDI data can be submitted to device registries per 21st Century Cures Act requirements
