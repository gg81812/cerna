2025 National Patient Safety Goals (NPSGs) — Hospital Program
Source: https://www.americandatanetwork.com/patient-safety/national-patient-safety-goals-2025/ (Joint Commission content)
Effective: January 1, 2025
Scraped: 2026-04-19

Note: Effective January 1, 2026, NPSGs will be replaced with National Performance Goals (NPGs).

==============================================================

NPSG #1: Identify Patients Correctly

Goal: Use at least two patient identifiers when providing care, treatment, and services.

Key Requirements:
- Apply dual identifiers during medication administration, blood/specimen collection, and procedures
- Never use room numbers or physical location as identifiers
- Label specimen containers in the patient's presence
- Implement distinct identification methods for newborns

Implementation Strategies:
- Deploy barcode medication administration (BCMA) systems (reduces errors by 57.5% per research)
- Verify identifiers at bedside during specimen labeling
- Flag look-alike/sound-alike names in EHR systems
- Encourage patient participation in identity verification
- Integrate patient photographs into EHR systems
- Conduct regular compliance audits

Cerner Implementation: Cerner BCMA workflow, patient photo integration in PowerChart, duplicate MRN alerts.

NPSG #2: Improve Staff Communication

Goal: Report critical results of tests and diagnostic procedures on a timely basis.

Key Requirements:
- Develop written procedures for managing critical results
- Define critical result definitions and reporting timeframes
- Identify reporting recipients and responsible parties
- Evaluate timeliness of critical result communication

Implementation Strategies:
- Implement structured handoff protocols (I-PASS methodology)
- Establish closed-loop communication for critical findings
- Use secure texting for result notification (reduces from 11.3 minutes to 3.0 minutes)
- Require read-back verification for verbal orders
- Conduct TeamSTEPPS training
- Deploy AI-driven alert systems for critical value escalation

Cerner Implementation: PowerChart critical result alerts, secure messaging, handoff report configuration.

NPSG #3: Use Medicines Safely

Goal: Label all medications and solutions; manage anticoagulant therapy safely; maintain accurate medication information.

Medication Labeling (NPSG 03.04.01):
- Label medications on and off sterile fields in procedural settings
- Include: medication name, strength, amount, diluent, expiration
- Verify labels verbally and visually by two qualified individuals
- Label immediately upon preparation (unless instantly administered)

Anticoagulant Therapy (NPSG 03.05.01):
- Use approved, evidence-based protocols for anticoagulation management
- Implement reversal protocols and bleeding management guidelines
- Utilize perioperative management protocols for oral anticoagulants
- Prefer unit-dose products, prefilled syringes, or premixed infusions

Medication Reconciliation (NPSG 03.06.01):
- Obtain complete medication history upon admission/initial visit
- Compare home medications with hospital orders; resolve discrepancies
- Provide discharge medication lists with education
- Explain medication management importance to patients

Implementation Strategies:
- Pharmacy-led medication reconciliation (reduces discrepancies from 90% to 47%)
- Deploy BCMA and smart infusion pumps with dose-error reduction
- Use automated dispensing cabinets with safety alerts
- Educate patients on high-risk medications

Cerner Implementation: PharmNet anticoagulation protocols, BCMA, discharge medication reconciliation workflow, Best Practice Advisories for missing reconciliation.

NPSG #4: Use Alarms Safely

Goal: Improve the safety of clinical alarm systems.

Key Requirements:
- Establish alarm safety as leadership priority
- Identify most important alarm signals based on clinical risk
- Develop policies addressing alarm settings, disabling, and monitoring
- Check alarm signals for accurate settings and proper operation

Implementation Strategies:
- Convene multidisciplinary alarm management committee
- Develop evidence-based policies to reduce non-actionable alarms
- Customize alarm thresholds to individual patient conditions
- Route critical alarms to responsible staff via smartphones/pagers
- Implement alarm analytics dashboards
- Deploy predictive AI tools for deterioration detection

Cerner Implementation: CareAware medical device integration, alarm routing configuration in Cerner Millennium.

NPSG #5: Prevent Infection

Goal: Comply with CDC or WHO hand hygiene guidelines.

Key Requirements:
- Implement program following CDC/WHO guideline categories IA, IB, IC
- Set goals for improving compliance
- Improve compliance based on established goals

Implementation Strategies:
- Adhere to "5 Moments for Hand Hygiene" guidelines
- Employ electronic monitoring or observational audits
- Provide immediate, specific compliance feedback
- Establish compliance targets (e.g., >90% hospital-wide)

NPSG #6: Reduce Risk for Suicide

Goal: Reduce the risk for suicide.

Key Requirements:
- Conduct environmental risk assessment
- Screen all psychiatric/behavioral health patients for suicidal ideation (age 12+)
- Use evidence-based assessment tools (e.g., Columbia-Suicide Severity Rating Scale, PHQ-9)
- Document suicide risk level and mitigation plan
- Provide counseling and follow-up care information at discharge

Implementation Strategies:
- Conduct thorough suicide risk assessments
- Use ligature-resistant rooms; remove environmental hazards
- Perform frequent risk re-assessments
- Develop personalized safety plans for discharge
- Post-discharge follow-up within 24-48 hours (significantly reduces suicide rates)

Cerner Implementation: PHQ-9 and Columbia screening integrated in FirstNet and PowerChart flowsheets, BPA alerts for incomplete suicide screening.

NPSG #7: Improve Health Care Equity

Goal: Improving healthcare equity is a quality and safety priority.

Key Requirements:
- Designate individual(s) to lead equity improvement activities
- Assess patients' health-related social needs (HRSNs)
- Identify disparities by stratifying quality/safety data by sociodemographic characteristics
- Develop written action plan addressing at least one identified disparity
- Annually inform stakeholders about equity improvement progress

Implementation Strategies:
- Stratify key quality/safety data by race, ethnicity, language, insurance type
- Screen patients for social determinants of health (SDOH)
- Ensure interpreter services availability
- Publish annual equity reports with stratified data

Cerner Implementation: SDOH screening documentation in PowerChart, population health dashboards for equity metrics, interpreter service tracking.

NPSG #8: Prevent Mistakes in Surgery

Goal: Conduct preprocedure verification; mark procedure sites; perform time-out before procedure.

Preprocedure Verification (UP 01.01.01):
- Verify correct procedure for correct patient at correct site
- Involve patient in verification when possible
- Verify availability of required documentation (history/physical, consent, assessments)
- Verify labeled diagnostic/radiology results are properly displayed

Procedure Site Marking (UP 01.02.01):
- Mark sites when multiple locations exist
- Marking performed by licensed practitioner accountable for procedure
- Mark at or near site with sufficiently permanent mark visible after skin preparation

Time-Out (UP 01.03.01):
- Conduct standardized time-out immediately before invasive procedure/incision
- Initiated by designated team member
- Team agrees on: correct patient identity, correct site, procedure to be done
- Document time-out completion

Implementation Strategies:
- Use standardized preprocedure verification checklist
- Adopt WHO Surgical Safety Checklist (19 items)
- Promote culture allowing any team member to speak up
- Audit time-out completion regularly

Cerner Implementation: SurgiNet perioperative workflows include time-out documentation, surgical safety checklist, patient identification verification at each phase.
