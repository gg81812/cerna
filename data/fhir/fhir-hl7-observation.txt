FHIR v4.0.1 Observation Resource
Source: https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/observation.html
Scraped: 2026-04-18

==============================================================

Overview

The Observation resource represents "measurements and simple assertions made about a patient, device or other subject." It serves as a central element in healthcare for supporting diagnosis, monitoring progress, and determining baselines and patterns.

Resource Status

- Maturity Level: Normative (from v4.0.0)
- Security Category: Patient
- Standards: ANSI-approved
- Current Version: Release 4 (R4); newer version 5.0.0 available

Scope and Usage

The Observation resource functions as an event resource within FHIR workflows. Common applications include:

- Vital signs (body weight, blood pressure, temperature)
- Laboratory data (blood glucose, estimated GFR)
- Imaging results (bone density, fetal measurements)
- Clinical findings (abdominal tenderness)
- Device measurements (EKG data, pulse oximetry)
- Clinical assessment tools (APGAR scores, Glasgow Coma Scale)
- Personal characteristics (eye color)
- Social history and core characteristics

Key Boundaries

Observation is specifically designed for measurements and point-in-time assessments. It should not replace specialized resources like AllergyIntolerance, MedicationStatement, FamilyMemberHistory, Procedure, or Condition for their respective use cases.

The DiagnosticReport resource provides clinical context for sets of observations rather than replacing Observation itself.

Core Elements

Required Fields:
- status: registered | preliminary | final | amended
- code: What was observed (CodeableConcept)

Key Optional Elements:
- identifier: Business identifier
- basedOn: Fulfilling plan/proposal/order
- subject: Patient, group, device, or location
- effective[x]: Observation timing (dateTime, Period, Timing, or instant)
- performer: Who performed the observation
- value[x]: Result (supports 11 different data types)
- interpretation: High, low, normal assessment
- referenceRange: Normal/recommended range guidance
- component: Multiple component observations (for composite results like blood pressure)

Data Types Supported

The value[x] field flexibly accepts:
Quantity, CodeableConcept, string, boolean, integer, Range, Ratio, SampledData, time, dateTime, Period

Reference Ranges

Observations support guidance through reference ranges with:
- Low and high bounds
- Applicability codes (population, age)
- Textual descriptions for non-quantitative results

Related Resources

Observation connects to numerous FHIR resources including Condition, DiagnosticReport, MedicationRequest, Procedure, and many others to provide comprehensive clinical context.

Profiles

The Vital Signs profile establishes minimum expectations for recording vital sign observations with standardized structure.
