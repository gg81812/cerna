Overview

The CDC provides immunization schedule resources specifically designed for healthcare providers to guide vaccination recommendations across different age groups.

Key Resources

Child and Adolescent Immunization Schedule by Age
- Last updated July 2, 2025
- Purpose: "Guide health care providers in determining recommended vaccines for each age group"
- Covers: Birth through 18 years
- Approved by: Advisory Committee on Immunization Practices (ACIP), American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP), American Academy of Family Physicians (AAFP)

Adult Immunization Schedule by Age
- Last updated July 2, 2025
- Purpose: Helps providers remain current on vaccination recommendations
- Covers: 19 years and older
- Approved by: ACIP, American College of Physicians (ACP), AAFP, American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists (ACOG)

Additional Vaccination Resources

- Easy-to-read schedules for both children and adults
- Adult vaccination information
- Pregnancy and vaccination guidance
- Vaccines for children
- Travel vaccine information
- Mpox vaccination resources

Contact Information

Providers can reach CDC at 800-232-4636 for additional immunization questions.
