# Cerner PowerChart Results Review and InBasket Guide

## Results Review in PowerChart

### Accessing Results

From a patient's chart in PowerChart:
1. Click "Results" in the chart navigation bar.
2. Results are organized by category:
   - **Labs** — all laboratory results
   - **Radiology** — imaging reports
   - **Vital Signs** — documented vital signs
   - **Documents** — transcribed reports and external documents
   - **Other** — specialty results (cardiology, pulmonary, etc.)

### Lab Results View

The lab results section offers multiple views:

**List View:**
- Chronological list of all lab results
- Each result shows: test name, result value, reference range, flag (Normal, Abnormal Low/High, Critical Low/High), collection date/time, result date/time, ordering provider

**Flowsheet View:**
- Time-based grid showing selected lab panels across multiple collection dates
- Ideal for trending: BMP across 5 days, CBC trend during sepsis treatment
- Columns = collection dates; Rows = individual lab tests

**Graph View:**
- Trend line chart for a single lab value over time
- Useful for: trending creatinine during AKI recovery, INR during warfarin therapy, hemoglobin during GI bleed management

### Critical Value Notification

When a lab result is flagged as Critical:
1. The lab system notifies the ordering provider via phone call (regulatory requirement).
2. A critical result notification appears in the provider's InBasket.
3. The result appears with a red "Critical" flag in the Results view.
4. The provider must acknowledge the critical result — documenting that they were aware and what action was taken.

Unacknowledged critical results generate repeat InBasket notifications and may escalate to the charge nurse or attending if the ordering provider is not reachable.

### Radiology Results

Radiology reports are transcribed by radiologists in the RIS (Radiology Information System) and transmitted to PowerChart via HL7 interface:
- Report appears in the Results > Radiology section.
- Report status: Preliminary (transcribed, not finalized) vs. Final (radiologist attested).
- For critical imaging findings, the radiologist contacts the ordering provider directly (per Joint Commission requirements) AND files a critical result in the InBasket.

### Vital Signs Flowsheet

1. Navigate to Results > Vital Signs.
2. View: Temperature, Heart Rate, Blood Pressure (systolic/diastolic), Respiratory Rate, SpO2, Weight.
3. Select a date range to see the trend.
4. Switch to Graph View to visualize trends.

## The InBasket (Message Center)

### What is the InBasket?

The InBasket (Message Center) is PowerChart's integrated inbox for all clinical communication requiring provider action. It functions like a secure, role-aware email system connected to clinical workflows.

Access:
- Click the "Message Center" or "InBasket" icon in the PowerChart toolbar.
- Or access from the PowerChart home screen (InBasket panel).

### InBasket Folder Types

**Results:**
- Lab results routed to the ordering provider for review and signature.
- Critical result notifications requiring immediate acknowledgment.
- Radiology reports requiring provider acknowledgment.

**Orders:**
- Verbal/telephone order cosignature requests — unsigned verbal orders from nursing staff awaiting provider signature.
- CPOE-related notifications (e.g., pharmacy-modified an order — requires provider awareness).

**Documents:**
- Notes awaiting cosignature (resident notes, student notes pending attending co-sign).
- Notes signed by consulting providers that require the primary team's awareness.

**Patient Messages:**
- Messages from patients via MyChart (the patient portal).
- Secure, asynchronous messaging allowing patients to ask non-urgent questions.

**Prescription Refills (Ambulatory):**
- Refill requests submitted by the patient's outpatient pharmacy via SureScripts.
- Provider reviews and approves/denies electronically.

**Administrative:**
- System-generated administrative alerts.
- Peer review requests.
- Quality improvement notifications.

### Processing InBasket Messages

#### Reviewing and Acknowledging Lab Results

1. Open the Results folder in the InBasket.
2. Click on a pending result.
3. Review the result value and clinical context.
4. Select an action:
   - **Sign** — provider acknowledges the result (appears in the patient's chart as reviewed).
   - **Sign and Forward** — review and forward to a colleague (e.g., referring the result to a specialist).
   - **Sign and Create Note** — review and immediately open a new note referencing the result.
   - **Delegate** — route to another provider (use for results outside your scope or when covering for a colleague).
5. Each acknowledged result is stamped with provider name and timestamp.

#### Cosigning Verbal Orders

1. Open the Orders folder.
2. Click on the pending verbal order.
3. Review the order details (medication, dose, route, frequency).
4. If correct: click "Sign" to cosign.
5. If incorrect: click "Modify" to correct before signing.

#### Responding to Patient Messages (MyChart)

1. Open the Patient Messages folder.
2. Read the patient's message.
3. Compose a reply in the secure messaging interface.
4. Click "Send" — the reply is delivered to the patient's MyChart inbox.
5. Clinical staff may be designated to triage patient messages and escalate to providers when clinical judgment is needed.

### InBasket Management Best Practices

**Pool / Shared InBaskets:**
For practices and clinical services, shared InBaskets allow a pool of providers to manage messages collectively:
- All providers in the pool see the same messages.
- The first provider to act on a message removes it from others' queues.
- Useful for on-call coverage and team-based care models.

**Coverage / Proxy Settings:**
When a provider is on vacation or unavailable:
1. Open InBasket > Settings > Coverage.
2. Set a proxy — InBasket messages are forwarded to the designated covering provider.
3. Set an auto-reply notification for patient messages during the coverage period.

### InBasket Overflow and Burnout

InBasket volume is a significant contributor to physician burnout. Best practices for managing InBasket burden:

1. **Delegate appropriately** — medical assistants and nurses can manage routine result notifications and patient messages under provider supervision.
2. **Implement message triaging** — clinical staff screens messages and elevates only those requiring physician decision-making.
3. **Set result routing rules** — configure result routing so routine normal results go to a pool rather than directly to the ordering provider.
4. **Batch InBasket time** — schedule dedicated InBasket processing time rather than interrupting clinical workflow.
5. **Monitor InBasket age** — generate reports showing average time to acknowledge results and messages; target <24 hours for routine results, <1 hour for critical.

## Reporting

Key Results and InBasket reports via Discern Analytics:
- Unacknowledged critical results by provider
- InBasket message age distribution
- Average time to critical result acknowledgment
- Verbal order cosignature compliance rate
- Results InBasket volume by provider and message type
