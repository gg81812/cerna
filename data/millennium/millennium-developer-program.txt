                    <p>Oracle Health (Cerner Millennium) is the most widely used EPR in NHS Trusts in England, with 35 NHS Trusts (at the time of writing this post) having implemented Oracle Health EHR (Cerner Millennium).</p>
<p>Integrating solutions with Oracle Health is critical for enhancing care delivery and operational efficiency, and the Oracle Health EHR (Cerner Millennium) Developer Program is designed to support digital health innovators with integration, providing a robust platform for developing, testing, and commercialising applications that integrate with Oracle Health.</p>
<p>With access to Oracle Health Millennium API documentation, best practice guidelines, and a testing sandbox, developers can leverage the Developer Program to experiment and refine their solutions before bringing them to market. This support helps streamline the development process, reducing time-to-market for new healthcare applications.</p>
<h2 id="section-unlocking-the-power-of-cerner-apis-and-tools-0">Unlocking the Power of Cerner APIs and Tools</h2>
<p>APIs are the backbone of modern healthcare IT systems, offering powerful opportunities for transforming care delivery. The Oracle Health Developer Program documents API capabilities native to the Millennium Platform, enabling developers to build their own applications or integrate with independent software vendors. This open ecosystem approach not only fosters innovation but also ensures that healthcare organisations can tailor their technology solutions to meet specific needs.</p>
<p>The Oracle Health Developer Program also provides access to a wealth of resources designed to support developers at every stage of their journey. This includes detailed API documentation, best practices, and a suite of developer tools. These resources are complemented by training materials, enabling developers to have the knowledge and skills needed to effectively utilise the Oracle Health Millennium Platform.</p>
<h2 id="section-the-oracle-health-developer-program-1">The Oracle Health Developer Program</h2>
<p>At the heart of the Oracle Health Developer Program is a vibrant developer community. This forum offers a space for developers to share technical questions, collaborate on solutions, and learn from one another. This spirit of open collaboration is key to driving continuous innovation and improvement within the Oracle Health ecosystem.</p>
<p>The Oracle Health Developer Program also provides comprehensive development resources, including documentation, education, and information necessary for connecting apps and devices to the Oracle Health Millennium Platform. Developers can browse various versions of documentation for the HL7 FHIR standard, including DSTU 2 and R4 implementations. Additionally, the program includes resources on SMART documentation and numerous open-source FHIR implementations, supporting a wide range of development needs.</p>
<h2 id="section-path-to-cerner-integration-commercialisation-2">Path to Cerner Integration Commercialisation</h2>
<p>Once an application is market-ready, the Oracle PartnerNetwork (accessed via the Developer Program) provides a streamlined path to commercialisation. This network helps developers gain the necessary skills and Oracle expertise to earn customer trust and relevancy.</p>
<p>By partnering with Oracle, and accessing the Oracle Developer Program, developers can leverage a well-established platform to reach a broader audience and ensure the successful deployment of their Oracle Health (Cerner Millennium) integrated solutions in real-world healthcare settings.</p>
