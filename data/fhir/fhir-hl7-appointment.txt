FHIR v4.0.1 Appointment Resource
Source: https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/appointment.html
Scraped: 2026-04-18

==============================================================

Overview

The Appointment resource represents "a booking of a healthcare event among patient(s), practitioner(s), related person(s) and/or device(s) for a specific date/time."

Key Purpose

Appointments document planned meetings that may occur in the future or past. They support various healthcare scenarios including scheduled surgeries, clinical follow-ups, clinician conferences, and equipment reservations. Visits can be in-person or remote.

Basic Workflow Steps

Discovery/Addressing: Determine contact details for the scheduling resource, typically via the Schedule resource.

Availability Check (Optional): Review available Slot resources linked to a Schedule to identify bookable times.

Appointment Request: A requester creates an Appointment with status "proposed" and participant status "needs-action."

Reply Process: Participants update their participation status. Multiple systems may generate AppointmentResponse entries.

Status Confirmation: The overall appointment status reflects participant responses using pub-sub techniques.

Waitlisting (Optional): Create waitlisted appointments for preferred times, notifying patients when slots become available.

Two Workflow Styles

Community Style: Select specific available times and make requests directly.

Clinical Style: Involves complex multi-system checking and timing considerations; requires queue-like processing.

Core Data Elements

- Status: proposed, pending, booked, arrived, fulfilled, cancelled, noshow, entered-in-error, checked-in, or waitlist
- Timing: Start/end dates and duration information
- Participants: Patients, practitioners, devices, locations with required/optional designation
- Reason: Clinical codes and resource references (conditions, procedures, observations)
- Service Details: Category, type, and specialty information
- Instructions: Patient-facing guidance separate from internal comments

Relationship to Other Resources

Appointments typically generate Encounters when services begin. The AppointmentResponse resource handles participant confirmations. Location participation is tracked through participant references rather than separate location fields.
