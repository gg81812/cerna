# Cerner FirstNet — Emergency Department Tracking and Clinical Workflows
# MODULE: Clinical
# SYNTHETIC KNOWLEDGE BASE — Cerna RAG System

## What Is FirstNet in Cerner?

FirstNet is Cerner Millennium's emergency department (ED) information system, providing an integrated tracking board, clinical documentation, CPOE order entry, nursing workflows, and patient throughput management specifically designed for the fast-paced emergency department care setting.

FirstNet is the primary clinical application for emergency nurses, physicians, and technicians in the ED. It provides a real-time, at-a-glance view of every patient in the emergency department, their status, assigned staff, pending orders, and acuity — all on a single configurable tracking board.

## FirstNet Tracking Board

The FirstNet tracking board is the central workspace for ED staff. It displays all current patients in the ED in a row-per-patient, column-per-data-point layout. The tracking board is fully configurable to display whatever data elements are most relevant to the ED's workflow.

### Standard Tracking Board Columns

| Column | Data Displayed |
|--------|---------------|
| Bed | Current bed or room assignment |
| Patient Name | Patient's name (may be masked based on privacy policy) |
| Acuity / ESI | Emergency Severity Index triage level (1-5) |
| Chief Complaint | Primary reason for visit |
| Time in ED | Elapsed time since registration |
| Physician | Assigned attending emergency physician |
| Nurse | Assigned primary nurse |
| Status | Current patient status (Triage, Assessment, Orders Pending, Results Pending, Ready for Discharge, etc.) |
| Orders | Count of pending or active orders |
| Results | Count of pending lab or radiology results |
| Last Update | Time of last clinical documentation update |

### Color-Coded Status Indicators
FirstNet uses color coding on the tracking board to communicate urgency:
- **Red cells**: critical patients (ESI 1), abnormal results pending, or overdue tasks
- **Yellow cells**: medium acuity (ESI 2-3), outstanding orders, or items needing attention
- **Green cells**: lower acuity (ESI 4-5), results returned, or patient ready for disposition
- **Gray cells**: boarding patients awaiting inpatient bed or transfer
- **Blue cells**: patients in isolation precautions

## Triage Workflow in FirstNet

### Step 1: Patient Registration
1. Patient arrives at ED — registration staff create encounter in Cerner (or auto-created from pre-hospital ADT)
2. Patient appears on tracking board with "Registered" status
3. Encounter type: EMERGENCY is automatically applied

### Step 2: Triage Assessment
1. Triage nurse selects the patient from the tracking board
2. Opens the **Triage PowerForm** within FirstNet
3. Documents:
   - Triage date/time
   - Emergency Severity Index (ESI) level 1-5
   - Chief complaint (coded and free-text)
   - Vital signs (BP, HR, RR, SpO2, temperature, pain score)
   - Allergy documentation
   - Triage medications (e.g., aspirin for chest pain per protocol)
4. Triage level automatically updates tracking board acuity column
5. Patient is assigned to a room/bed on the tracking board

### Step 3: Physician Assessment and CPOE
1. ED physician opens patient chart from tracking board
2. Documents History of Present Illness (HPI) in PowerNote or dictation
3. Enters orders via CPOE:
   - Laboratory orders (auto-routed to lab via HL7)
   - Imaging orders (auto-routed to radiology)
   - Medications (auto-routed to pharmacy/eMAR)
   - Nursing orders (tasks appear in nursing task list)
4. Order status updates reflected in tracking board Orders column in real time

### Step 4: Nursing Care Documentation
1. Nurse completes nursing assessments in PowerForms:
   - Head-to-toe nursing assessment
   - Pain assessment
   - Fall risk (Morse Fall Scale)
   - Suicide risk screen
2. Administers medications via eMAR/BCMA workflow (same as inpatient)
3. Documents IV access, patient education, and care coordination notes
4. Updates patient status on tracking board (drag-and-drop or status button)

### Step 5: Results Review
1. Lab results auto-populate in patient chart Results section
2. Critical values trigger a **critical result notification** alert to the ordering physician via InBasket
3. Radiology results with preliminary reads appear in chart immediately; final reads update automatically
4. Physician reviews results and documents assessment update in chart

### Step 6: Disposition Decision
After evaluation, physician makes disposition:
- **Discharge**: Discharge order placed → discharge instructions generated in PowerChart
- **Admission**: Admission order placed → bed request sent to bed management → patient moves to inpatient workflow
- **Transfer**: Transfer order placed → coordination with receiving facility via HL7 or secure message
- **Observation**: Observation order placed → encounter type may change to OBSERVATION

## FirstNet-Specific Features

### Nursing Track Board (NTB)
A nursing-specific view of the tracking board that shows:
- Outstanding nursing tasks by patient
- Medication administration due times
- Patient flow status indicators

### Quick List Orders
ED physicians can configure personal **Quick Lists** — pre-defined order sets or individual orders accessible from the tracking board with a single click:
- "Chest Pain Work-up" — troponin, EKG order, aspirin, oxygen
- "Abdominal Pain Panel" — CBC, CMP, lipase, UA, IV access
- "Pediatric Fever" — CBC, blood culture, UA, urine culture

### Protocol Orders (Standing Orders)
Triage nurses can place **standing protocol orders** at triage before physician evaluation:
- Examples: Chest pain protocol, sepsis screening orders, stroke activation orders
- Configured in: **FirstNet Administration > Triage Protocol Orders**
- Requires nursing license scope validation in your state

### Track Board Filters and Views
The tracking board supports multiple simultaneous views:
- **All Patients**: complete ED census
- **By Zone**: grouped by ED zone (Fast Track, Trauma, Medical)
- **My Patients**: filtered to current user's assigned patients
- **Boarding**: patients with inpatient admission pending bed assignment
- **Rooms**: room status view for bed management

## FirstNet Administration and Configuration

### Accessing FirstNet Administration
1. Navigate to **Cerner Solutions > FirstNet Administration**
2. Or: launch FirstNet > Administration ribbon tab (requires admin security access)

### Configuring the Tracking Board
1. **Column Configuration**: add, remove, or reorder tracking board columns
2. **Color Rules**: define when cells change color (threshold-based on time, result status, or custom criteria)
3. **Status Labels**: customize the patient status values shown in the Status column
4. **Sort Order**: configure default sort (by acuity, by time in ED, or by zone)

### Configuring Triage PowerForms
1. Navigate to **PowerForms Administration > Triage Assessment**
2. Add or modify form sections (vital sign sections, chief complaint sections)
3. Map form fields to clinical event codes for data extraction in CCL reports

### Configuring Quick List Orders
1. Access **FirstNet Administration > Quick Order Lists**
2. Create ED-specific order groupings by acuity level and chief complaint
3. Assign to triage protocols or physician preference lists

## Patient Safety Features in FirstNet

- **Wristband printing**: auto-prints patient ID wristband at triage registration with barcode for BCMA scanning
- **Isolation alerts**: tracking board flags patients in contact, droplet, or airborne isolation
- **Violence risk flags**: security alerts visible to all staff for patients with behavioral concerns
- **Diversion tracking**: monitors when ED is on diversion status and alerts bed management

## Reporting and Analytics for the ED

FirstNet feeds key ED metrics to Discern Analytics:
- **Door-to-provider time**: time from registration to first physician contact
- **Length of stay (LOS)**: total time from arrival to disposition
- **Left without being seen (LWBS)**: patients who leave before evaluation
- **Admit-to-bed time**: time from admission decision to inpatient bed assignment
- **Boarding time**: time inpatient patients spend in ED waiting for a bed

Access ED dashboards: **Discern Analytics > Emergency Department Performance Dashboard**
