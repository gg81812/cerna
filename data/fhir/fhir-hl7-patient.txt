FHIR v4.0.1 Patient Resource
Source: https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/patient.html
Scraped: 2026-04-18

==============================================================

Overview

The Patient resource documents "Demographics and other administrative information about an individual or animal receiving care or other health-related services."

Scope and Coverage

This resource addresses patient data across multiple healthcare contexts:
- Curative activities
- Psychiatric care
- Social services
- Pregnancy care
- Nursing and assisted living
- Dietary services
- Personal health and exercise tracking

The resource focuses on "who" information — demographic details needed for administrative, financial, and logistical procedures. Organizations typically maintain patient records, meaning individuals receiving care at multiple facilities may have multiple Patient resource instances.

Key Limitations

Not all demographic concepts appear in the base resource (race, ethnicity, organ donor status, nationality). Such fields exist in jurisdiction-specific profiles or standard extensions, as requirements and value sets vary significantly across regions.

Primary Data Elements

Administrative Elements:
- Identifiers (0 or more)
- Active status (0 or 1)
- Names (0 or more)
- Contact points/telecom (0 or more)
- Gender (0 or 1)
- Birth date (0 or 1)
- Deceased status (0 or 1, as boolean or dateTime)

Address and Status:
- Addresses (0 or more)
- Marital status (0 or 1)
- Multiple birth indicator (0 or 1)
- Photos (0 or more)

Relationship and Care:
- Contact parties: guardians, partners, friends (0 or more)
- Communication languages with preference flags (0 or more)
- General practitioners (0 or more)
- Managing organization (0 or 1)
- Patient links referencing related Patient or RelatedPerson records (0 or more)

Maturity Status

The Patient resource achieved Normative status beginning with version 4.0.0, indicating stable, standardized specifications approved as an ANSI standard.
