What is a CPT Code?

The Current Procedural Terminology (CPT) code set consists of "five-digit codes that primarily describe medical services and procedures performed by physicians and other qualified health care professionals." These codes now encompass algorithm-enabled services and care team provider services as well.

CPT codes function as standardized language for reporting physician-patient interactions across U.S. healthcare, enabling interoperability among practices, health systems, and payers. They support value-based care models and help track health care technologies while providing data for measurement and benchmarking.

Types of CPT Codes

Category I codes (00100–99499): Numeric codes corresponding to established procedures or services, organized by type and anatomy. The primary codes used for billing.

Category II codes: Alphanumeric tracking codes used for performance measurement to facilitate data collection on care quality and test results supporting nationally established performance measures.

Category III codes: Temporary alphanumeric codes for emerging technology, procedures, and services, allowing identification of new technologies for evaluation of clinical efficacy and utilization.

PLA codes: Describe proprietary clinical laboratory analyses, including Advanced Diagnostic Laboratory Tests (ADLTs) and Clinical Diagnostic Laboratory Tests (CDLTs) as defined under PAMA.

Code Development and Maintenance

The independent CPT Editorial Panel, appointed by the AMA Board of Trustees, maintains and updates the CPT code set. Panel members work with CPT Advisors — physicians nominated by national medical specialty societies — to ensure evidence-based review of code changes.

The Panel meets three times yearly to review applications for new codes or revisions. Stakeholders can attend meetings or submit applications for code modifications.

CPT Code Structure

Format: 5-digit numeric codes (Category I) or alphanumeric (Category II/III)
Categories in Category I:
- Evaluation and Management (99202–99499)
- Anesthesia (00100–01999)
- Surgery (10004–69990)
- Radiology (70010–79999)
- Pathology and Laboratory (80047–89398)
- Medicine (90281–99199)

Evolution with Medicine

The CPT code set evolves alongside clinical innovations in genomics, digital medicine, and AI-enabled services.
