Overview

The American Nurses Association (ANA) develops position statements addressing nursing practice, health policy, and healthcare-related social concerns. The organization follows a formal process: appointing Professional Issues Panels to research topics, posting drafts for public comment, and securing Board of Directors approval.

Development Process

The ANA's methodology:
1. ANA Board of Directors approves a relevant topic
2. ANA Professional Issues Panel is appointed to research and draft a position
3. Draft is posted for public review and comment
4. Board of Directors approves final statement

ANA Informatics Nursing Standards

The ANA publishes Nursing Informatics: Scope and Standards of Practice, which establishes:
- Competencies for informatics nurse specialists
- Data, information, knowledge, and wisdom framework (DIKW)
- Requirements for EHR system engagement
- Expectations for clinical decision support participation
- Standards for workflow analysis and redesign

Relevance to Cerner Implementations

Nursing informatics standards directly impact Cerner configuration:
- Documentation requirements drive PowerChart flowsheet and form design
- Nursing care plan standards inform CareCompass and ClinicalWorks configuration
- Delegation policies drive Cerner task list and escalation configuration
- Fatigue management informs shift duration display in scheduling modules
- BCMA (Barcode Medication Administration) workflows must align with safe medication administration standards
