It has developed a top-notch <a href="https://ehrenhancify.com/2023/11/09/cerner-emr-everything-you-need-to-know/">EHR</a> system called Millennium.</p>
<p>One of the most impressive features of Millennium is its MPages. In this article, we&#8217;ll explore what they are, dig into their purpose, and see how they&#8217;re making a real impact on healthcare. So, let&#8217;s get started.</p>
<h2>What are MPages in Oracle Health?</h2>
<p>MPages in Cerner (or Oracle Health) are specialized components that give you a comprehensive and up-to-the-minute view of patient information from different parts of the Oracle Health Millennium database. With MPages, you can create personalized views in PowerChart that wouldn&#8217;t be possible otherwise.</p>
<p>These are really important because they address the unique challenges that Oracle Health customers face: challenges which can&#8217;t be solved with a one-size-fits-all solution.</p>
<p>Standard MPages show clinical information by combining components from a standard library. But you can also create custom MPages for individual or multiple patient views. This gives you a lot of flexibility to configure things the way you want.</p>
<p>We&#8217;ll delve into this later.  Can’t wait? Trial our <a href="https://ehrenhancify.com/custommpage/">custom MPage Diabetes Predictor</a>.</p>
<h2>What are MPages’ purpose?</h2>
<p>MPages improve the workflow and efficiency of healthcare professionals, allowing them to focus more on patient care.</p>
