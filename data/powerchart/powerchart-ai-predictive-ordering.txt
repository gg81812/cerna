## What Is PowerChart AI?

PowerChart AI is Oracle Health's suite of artificial intelligence and machine learning capabilities embedded within the PowerChart clinical documentation environment. It encompasses predictive ordering, AI-assisted documentation, ambient clinical intelligence, and clinical decision support driven by machine learning models rather than rule-based logic.

PowerChart AI represents Oracle Health's integration of AI from the Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) AI Services platform into the Cerner Millennium clinical workflow.

## Predictive Ordering

### Overview
Predictive ordering uses machine learning to analyze historical ordering patterns and patient context to suggest the most likely next orders a clinician will want. Instead of a clinician searching through the order catalog, the system proactively surfaces high-probability orders in a "recommended orders" panel.

### How Predictive Ordering Works

The predictive ordering model:
1. Analyzes **current patient context**: diagnosis, age, sex, active medications, lab results, care setting
2. Compares against **historical ordering patterns**: what similar patients received and what was clinically appropriate
3. Applies **provider preference weighting**: individual provider's ordering history influences personalized suggestions
4. Surfaces **top N predicted orders** in the CPOE interface with confidence scores

### Configuration Steps for Predictive Ordering

**Prerequisites**:
- Oracle Health cloud contract including AI services entitlement
- OCI connectivity and data sharing agreement executed
- Minimum historical data requirement: 12+ months of ordering data in the instance

**Step 1: Enable Predictive Ordering Feature Flag**
1. Navigate to **PowerChart Administration > AI Settings > Predictive Ordering**
2. Enable the feature toggle for your domain
3. Select data scope: facility-level, provider-level, or system-wide model

**Step 2: Configure Order Panel Display**
1. In **PowerChart Administration > CPOE Layout**, add the "AI Suggested Orders" panel
2. Position the panel in the CPOE workflow (typically above the order search field)
3. Set the number of suggested orders to display (recommended: 5-10)
4. Configure the confidence threshold for display (minimum 70% confidence)

**Step 3: Configure Model Training Parameters**
1. Navigate to **AI Administration > Model Configuration > Predictive Ordering**
2. Select training population:
   - Encounter type filter (inpatient, outpatient, emergency)
   - Care setting filter (ICU, medical-surgical, oncology)
   - Time window for training data (rolling 12 months recommended)
3. Enable **provider personalization**: model learns individual provider ordering preferences
4. Set model refresh cadence (weekly retraining recommended)

**Step 4: Configure Clinical Safety Guardrails**
1. Define **excluded order classes**: ensure the AI does not suggest controlled substances, chemotherapy, or high-alert medications without explicit criteria
2. Enable **allergy and interaction checking** for all AI-suggested orders (same CDS checks as manual orders)
3. Set **confidence minimum threshold**: orders below this threshold are suppressed from suggestions

**Step 5: Monitor and Tune the Model**
- Review **Predictive Ordering Analytics** in OCI AI dashboard weekly for first 90 days
- Track: suggestion acceptance rate, top accepted vs. rejected suggestions, false positive rate
- Tune confidence thresholds based on clinician feedback

### Predictive Ordering in the PowerChart Workflow

In the CPOE order entry workflow:
1. Clinician opens a patient chart and navigates to Order Entry
2. **AI Suggested Orders** panel displays top predicted orders with confidence percentages
3. Clinician can click individual suggested orders to add them to the order list
4. Selected orders go through standard CPOE workflow (CDS checks, sign, verify)
5. Clinician can also search catalog normally — AI suggestions are additive, not mandatory

## Ambient Clinical Intelligence (ACI)

### Overview
Ambient Clinical Intelligence (also called AI Scribe or voice-to-documentation) captures the clinician-patient conversation using secure audio processing and automatically drafts clinical documentation — including SOAP notes, H&P sections, and assessment/plan — within PowerNote.

### How ACI Works
1. Clinician activates the ambient microphone (via tablet, dedicated device, or integrated room mic)
2. The conversation is transcribed and processed by Oracle's AI models
3. Structured clinical data is extracted: HPI, ROS, exam findings, assessment, plan
4. A draft note is inserted into PowerNote using the appropriate template
5. The clinician reviews, edits, and signs the AI-generated note

### Configuration Steps for Ambient Clinical Intelligence

**Step 1: Enable ACI in PowerChart Administration**
1. Navigate to **Administration > AI Settings > Ambient Clinical Intelligence**
2. Enable the feature for selected provider roles and care settings
3. Configure the compatible PowerNote template (must support ACI placeholders)

**Step 2: Set Up Audio Capture Device Integration**
- Compatible devices: Nuance DAX, Oracle AI Scribe microphone, iPad with PowerChart app
- Configure device pairing in **Device Management > ACI Audio Devices**
- Ensure HIPAA-compliant audio storage and processing agreements are in place

**Step 3: Configure Documentation Template Mapping**
1. Navigate to **PowerNote Administration > ACI Template Mapping**
2. Map AI output fields to PowerNote sections:
   - Chief Complaint → CC section
   - HPI narrative → HPI section
   - Review of Systems → ROS section
   - Physical Exam findings → Exam section
   - Assessment → Assessment section
   - Plan (orders, follow-up) → Plan section
3. Configure which fields require human review before auto-population
4. Set fields as "AI Suggested" (amber color) to indicate clinician review needed

**Step 4: Provider Training and Enablement**
- Train providers on how to activate/deactivate ACI during the encounter
- Explain patient consent workflow (must inform patient of ambient recording)
- Provide guidance on reviewing and editing AI-generated content

## Machine Learning in Clinical Decision Support

PowerChart AI also integrates ML-based alerts that differ from traditional rule-based CDS:

### Sepsis Prediction Model
- Analyzes vital signs, lab trends, and medication orders continuously
- Predicts sepsis risk score (0-100) for each inpatient
- Fires advisory alert at configurable score threshold (typically 70+)
- Configuration: **CDS Administration > ML Rules > Sepsis Prediction**

### Readmission Risk Scoring
- ML model assesses 30-day readmission probability at discharge
- Integrates with discharge planning workflow in PowerChart
- High-risk patients flagged for social work and case management review
- Configuration: **Administration > AI Settings > Readmission Risk**

### Deterioration Index (DI)
- Continuous monitoring of vital signs for early deterioration detection
- Alerts nursing team before overt clinical decompensation
- Especially useful in step-down and medical-surgical settings
- Based on validated modified early warning score (MEWS) augmented with ML

## PowerChart AI Best Practices

1. **Establish governance**: AI suggestions require clinical governance approval before go-live — include CMO, CMIO, CIO, and legal in planning
2. **Patient consent**: document your ambient AI consent process; some states require explicit patient consent for room audio capture
3. **Physician trust**: never force AI suggestions — keep them optional and clearly labeled as AI-generated
4. **Monitor bias**: regularly audit AI suggestion acceptance rates across different patient populations and provider types to detect potential bias
5. **Integration testing**: test all AI-suggested orders through full CPOE workflow including CDS checks
6. **Provider feedback loop**: build a mechanism for providers to flag inappropriate AI suggestions directly in the UI
