# Cerner Revenue Cycle Management — Overview and Module Map

## What is Cerner Revenue Cycle Management?

Cerner Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) is an integrated suite of applications within Cerner Millennium that manages the financial lifecycle of patient care — from pre-registration and insurance verification through clinical documentation, charge capture, coding, claims submission, and collections. Cerner's RCM philosophy is called CDRC — Clinically Driven Revenue Cycle — which connects clinical care delivery to financial outcomes, reducing manual processes and improving accuracy.

Cerner RCM is branded under the Oracle Health RevElate product suite in recent product naming.

## The Revenue Cycle Lifecycle

The revenue cycle follows the patient's journey from appointment scheduling through final payment:

1. **Pre-Registration** — demographic and insurance information collected before the patient arrives
2. **Registration / Patient Access** — full registration, insurance eligibility verification, financial counseling at point of service
3. **Clinical Encounter** — services delivered and documented (orders, procedures, diagnoses)
4. **Charge Capture** — billable services converted to charges based on clinical events
5. **Clinical Documentation Improvement (CDI)** — concurrent query of providers to improve diagnosis specificity and DRG accuracy
6. **Coding (HIM)** — coders assign ICD-10-CM diagnosis codes and CPT/ICD-10-PCS procedure codes
7. **Charge Review** — charges audited for accuracy before claim submission
8. **Claims Submission** — clean claims submitted to payers (Medicare, Medicaid, commercial insurers)
9. **Remittance / Payment Posting** — payer payments posted against account balances
10. **Denial Management** — denied claims analyzed and appealed
11. **Patient Billing** — patient statements generated and payment plans offered
12. **Collections** — outstanding balances pursued through payment plans, financial assistance programs, or collection agencies

## Cerner RCM Module Map

### Patient Access Modules

**Scheduling** — Appointment scheduling for outpatient and procedure-based visits.

**Registration (ADT)** — Patient registration and encounter creation; insurance and demographic data capture.

**Insurance Eligibility Verification** — Real-time insurance eligibility checks via clearinghouse integration (payer portals or 270/271 HL7 transactions).

**Financial Counseling** — Pre-service cost estimation, financial assistance screening (charity care eligibility), payment plan setup.

**Prior Authorization Management** — Tracking pre-authorization requirements by payer and procedure; documenting authorization numbers on encounters.

### Clinical Documentation and Charge Capture Modules

**CPOE (Computerized Provider Order Entry)** — Orders drive charge triggers automatically (procedure-based charging).

**Charge Services** — Converts clinical events (orders, procedures, services) into billable charge lines. Rule-based charge triggers reduce manual charge entry.

**Charge Review** — Workflow tool for charge review specialists to audit charge lines for completeness and accuracy before claim generation.

**Clinical Documentation Improvement (CDI)** — Concurrent review of documentation by CDI specialists; query workflow for physicians to clarify diagnosis specificity.

### Coding and Health Information Management (HIM)

**Enterprise Coding (HIM)** — Coding workstation where medical coders assign ICD-10-CM, CPT, and ICD-10-PCS codes to the medical record.

**Computer-Assisted Coding (CAC)** — AI/NLP technology that suggests ICD-10 and CPT codes based on clinical documentation, reducing coding time.

**DRG Management** — Tools for reviewing MS-DRG assignments for inpatient cases; optimizes reimbursement within documentation accuracy constraints.

### Claims and Billing Modules

**Claims Management** — Electronic claims generation (837P/837I transactions), scrubbing, submission to clearinghouse, and payer tracking.

**Remittance Management** — 835 electronic remittance advice (ERA) processing; automatic payment posting.

**Denial Management** — Denial tracking, root cause analysis, and appeal workflow management.

**Patient Accounting** — Inpatient and outpatient account management; patient statement generation; payment posting.

**Patient Billing / Estimates** — Self-pay billing, patient-friendly statements, online payment portal.

**Collections** — Bad debt management; integration with collection agency workflows.

### Analytics and Reporting

**HealtheAnalytics / RevElate** — Population-level financial analytics: AR aging, denial rates, days to payment, collection rates by payer, charge capture efficiency.

**Charge Audit Reports** — Identify missing charges, charge lag, and outlier charges.

**Quality and Compliance Reports** — Documentation completeness rates, CDI query response rates, coding accuracy.

## CDRC — Clinically Driven Revenue Cycle

CDRC is Cerner's philosophy that integrates clinical care with financial outcomes. Key principles:

1. **Clinical Event = Financial Event** — Every clinical action (order placed, service rendered, diagnosis documented) automatically triggers the appropriate financial event. Reduces manual charge entry and human error.

2. **Documentation Drives Revenue** — The specificity and completeness of clinical documentation directly determines DRG assignment and reimbursement. CDI ensures documentation meets this standard.

3. **Physician Engagement** — Physicians are engaged at the point of care (via CDI queries and CDS alerts) rather than after-the-fact, when documentation is harder to amend.

4. **Analytics-Driven Improvement** — HealtheAnalytics/RevElate dashboards surface revenue integrity opportunities, denial trends, and documentation gaps in real time.

## Integration Between Clinical and Revenue Cycle Systems

Cerner RCM is tightly integrated with the clinical Millennium applications:
- CPOE orders → Charge Capture (automatic charge trigger)
- Discharge diagnosis → CDI and Coding workflow initiation
- Documentation completion → HIM queue activation
- Payer contract terms → Real-time charge scrubbing rules
- Prior authorization number → Claims attachment

This integration is what differentiates Cerner RCM from standalone billing systems — the financial data is generated directly from clinical events, not re-keyed by billing staff.

## Best Practices

1. Configure automatic charge triggers for high-volume procedures to reduce manual charge entry lag.
2. Implement concurrent CDI review — CDI queries issued after discharge have lower physician response rates and lower documentation improvement rates.
3. Establish daily charge review workflow — charge lag (delay between service and charge submission) directly increases AR days.
4. Monitor denial rates weekly — early identification of systemic denial trends allows faster root cause resolution.
5. Measure and publish documentation specificity rates — physician engagement with CDI improves when they see their impact on DRG assignment.
